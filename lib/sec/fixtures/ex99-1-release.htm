HALCYON GRID SYSTEMS CORP

Halcyon Grid Systems Reports Record Third Quarter 2026 Revenue of $48.2 Million, Up 61% Year‑Over‑Year

Raises full‑year outlook; backlog grows to $310 million

 

AUSTIN, Texas, Nov. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Halcyon Grid Systems Corp (NYSE: HGRD) (“Halcyon” or the “Company”), a maker of grid‑scale battery storage systems, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Revenue was $48.2 million, compared with $29.9 million a year earlier — the Company’s highest quarterly revenue to date.

“We delivered more megawatt‑hours this quarter than in all of 2024,” said Dana Whitfield, Chief Executive Officer. “Demand from utilities & data‑center developers hasn’t slowed.”

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

•Revenue of $48.2 million, up 61%
•Gross margin of 27.4%, up from 21.0%
•Net loss of $(3.1) million, or $(0.04) per share
